💾 My C64 Story

The Commodore 64 wasn't just my first computer. It was where my passion for technology began.

When I was a kid, my dad bought our family a Commodore 64. I was just 7 years old when I started learning BASIC, typing in programs, experimenting with code, and discovering that you could actually make a computer do things yourself. Of course, there was plenty of gaming too! Loading games from cassette tapes, waiting patiently, and enjoying those unforgettable 8-bit graphics and SID soundtracks.

Looking back, that little beige machine played a huge part in shaping my interest in computers and, eventually, my career in IT.

Fast-forward more than four decades, and I found myself buying the new Commodore 64 Ultimate. And suddenly, the magic was back!

The familiar blue screen, the blinking cursor, the sound, the games, and that irresistible urge to start programming again.

🚀 Old School Meets New School

This repository is my little corner of 8-bit nostalgia.

A place to share Commodore 64 projects, experiments, games, and whatever else I come up with along the way. Some projects are inspired by the classics I grew up with, others combine retro computing with modern technology.

Expect a mix of BASIC, 6502 assembly, retro gaming, and probably some ideas that make absolutely no sense on a modern computer. But that's half the fun!

🕹️ Projects

Lazy Jones – Parallels 20-Year Anniversary Edition

My first project takes me right back to one of the games I loved playing as a kid: Lazy Jones.

Recreated as a personal tribute to celebrate 20 years of Parallels, this C64-native project was first showcased at the Parallels VIPP Summit in Malta in October 2026.

Built using 6502 assembly, it combines classic C64 gameplay with a modern anniversary twist.
